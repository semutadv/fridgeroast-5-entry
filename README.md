The fridge roasts back, in one line, for the make.fun bounty "Fridge
Roast One-Line". It has kept the records: the nightly openings, the
full minute of staring at food that has not changed, the hopeful
closing of the door. Its conclusion about its own job is the roast —
not a fridge at all, but a hope dispenser with a light bill.

## Running it

`node main.ts` clears the output directory's contents and writes the
line to `/output/entry.txt` as plain UTF-8 text.
